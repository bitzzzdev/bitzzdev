Two hours outside. One good frame.

The trick was not the lens. It was the waiting.

Light moved fast that morning. I had the camera down more than I had it up, which is the only way to catch anything at all.

What I would tell anyone starting out: bring less. A longer walk, a slower shutter, a better sense of what you are looking for.

The camera is just a way to remember what you already saw.
