The last ten percent of any edit is where the work actually happens.

Everyone talks about the first draft. The shaping, the chaos, the momentum. But sitting in the final pass, watching the details come together, is its own kind of quiet.

I keep a few rules when I get there.

First, read it out loud. Your ear catches what your eyes skip. Second, cut one thing every paragraph. Third, check the rhythm, not just the words. A good piece has a beat.

None of this is glamorous. That is the point.
